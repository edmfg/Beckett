This isn't a mockup of fake tech. Every image you see on this site is generated live by the production Gemini API — the same stack a creative team would run during the game.

Nano Banana Pro (gemini-3-pro-image-preview) renders multi-reference compositions — meme template + friend face + team jersey + stadium — in under 10 seconds, at pennies per image. The format is the product.

  1. The format is the product
    Live Contextual isn't a tactic. It's a new creative category — ads that change because the game changed.
