How it works

Signal in. Answer out.

No play-by-play sprint. A sustained game-state. When the broadcast goes close-and-late, the CTV ad fires — and the fan's phone becomes the second screen Google was built for.

FLOW · Signal → Trigger → Ad → Search
01

Four steps.
One handoff.

The whole system in a single line: NBCU reads the screen, a condition fires, the CTV ad drops a take, the fan's phone finishes the argument inside Google Search (AI Mode).

Step 01
01
Signal
NBCU's ACR/OCR feed watches the live broadcast and reads game-state — the score, the clock, the on-screen graphics.
Step 02
02
Trigger
A condition fires — game is close, game is late. Sustained state, not a single play. Blowouts filter themselves out.
Step 03
03
Ad
A contextual CTV ad serves on the same screen. It drops a take — evergreen, situation-aimed, never team-specific.
Step 04
04
Search
The viewer reaches for their phone. Google Search (AI Mode) answers before the replay ends.

Thresholds tune per sport: ~7 points in football, ~6 points in basketball; halftime or 4th quarter. Game-state beats single-play: a play is a 25-second latency sprint, a close score is a sustained condition with a comfortable window.
